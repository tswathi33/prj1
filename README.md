# College Companion App

A smart and user-friendly application designed to help college students manage academics, campus activities, and daily college life in one place.

---

## Project Overview

College Companion App is a centralized platform that simplifies student life by providing features like attendance tracking, timetable management, notices, study materials, and event updates.  
This application helps students stay organized and informed throughout their academic journey.

---

## Objectives

- Reduce dependency on manual notice boards  
- Help students track attendance and schedules easily  
- Provide quick access to study materials  
- Improve communication between students and college administration  

---

## Features

- Student Login and Profile Management  
- Timetable and Academic Calendar  
- Attendance Tracking  
- Notices and Announcements  
- Study Materials Sharing  
- Campus Map and Navigation  
- Event Management  
- Feedback and Grievance System
