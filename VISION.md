
# College Companion: Product Vision

## The Vision
To transform the fragmented college experience into a unified, AI-first ecosystem. College Companion isn't just an app; it's a "Digital Brain" for students that anticipates needs, simplifies administration, and amplifies learning through multi-modal intelligence.

## Target Users
1. **The Overwhelmed Student**: Needs to track attendance, deadlines, and find campus locations instantly.
2. **The Modern Faculty**: Wants to distribute materials, track engagement, and use AI to summarize research.
3. **The Efficiency-Driven Admin**: Needs a direct channel for urgent notices and campus-wide data insights.

## Core Value Proposition
- **Unified Interface**: One app for schedules, maps, and notes.
- **AI-Powered Productivity**: Gemini-driven summarization and visual creation.
- **Real-time Connectivity**: Instant notices and maps grounding.
